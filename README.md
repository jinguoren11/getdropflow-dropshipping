## How it works

1. **Connect your AliExpress account** — authorize with your own account and use your own API quota.
2. **Import and price automatically** — pull products into your Magento catalog; your pricing rules set every sale price.
3. **Order and track** — place the supplier order and pay on AliExpress yourself; tracking numbers sync back to your Magento orders.

## Features

- **Product import** — AliExpress products, images and variants as simple or configurable Magento products.
- **Pricing rules** — markup, fixed fee, minimum margin and rounding, by product, category or store-wide.
- **Risk screening** — flag listings with brand-name keywords, low ratings, low sales or very new shops before they go live.
- **Order and tracking sync** — create supplier orders from your store orders and bring tracking numbers back automatically.
- **Free Google Shopping and Pinterest product feed** — a product data file that follows your prices and stock; you paste its address into both services once, with your own accounts. Available in every plan.
- **Runs on your own server** — your products, orders and credentials stay in your own Magento.
- **Not limited to one niche.**

## What GetDropFlow does not do

- It never pays for orders automatically — you pay on AliExpress yourself.
- It does not copy AliExpress customer reviews.
- It does not take a cut of your sales.
